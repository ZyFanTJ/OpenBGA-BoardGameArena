# Mobile lobby reference, November 2026

`mobile-moon-hero-v1.png` was generated with the built-in imagegen tool using the user-provided mobile lobby mockup as the composition reference. Existing game covers are reused; names, player counts and availability come from the game registry.

Generation prompt:

> Extract and reconstruct only the cinematic background from the top hero section of the mobile website reference. Landscape 1536 × 1024, no UI. Preserve the dark near-black blue room on the left, window on the right, large warm full moon at x68% y28%, castle skyline over a lake, glowing brass lantern on a wooden game table at lower right, dice, cards and boxes. Remove all typography, icons and buttons. Keep the left half dark for overlaid copy; fade the bottom to #050e15. Photoreal cinematic navy and warm gold, no text or watermark.

The heading font is a small Google Fonts Noto Serif SC 700 subset, stored locally at `../fonts/noto-serif-sc-lobby.ttf`. Its SIL Open Font License is included alongside it. Only headline characters are included; other text uses the platform font stack.
